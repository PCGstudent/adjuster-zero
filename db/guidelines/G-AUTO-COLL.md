# G-AUTO-COLL — Collision Coverage

## Scope
Collision (AUTO-COLL) covers impact with another vehicle or object and rollover,
regardless of fault. Damage from hitting an animal is comprehensive, not
collision.

## Deductible
The collision deductible (commonly $500) applies per loss and is subtracted from
the settlement. Collision does not carry the glass-zero endorsement.

## Total loss
When repair cost exceeds the actual cash value (ACV) threshold, the claim is a
total loss and is settled at ACV less deductible, not repaired.
