# G-AUTO-COMP — Comprehensive Coverage

## Scope
Comprehensive (AUTO-COMP) covers non-collision losses: theft, vandalism, fire,
flooding, hail and other weather, falling objects, and animal strikes. Glass
losses fall under comprehensive.

## Deductible
A comprehensive deductible applies unless a specific endorsement (e.g.
GLASS-ZERO-DED) waives it for a covered sub-peril.

## Corroboration
Weather-related comprehensive losses (hail, wind, flood) should be corroborated
against an independent weather-event record for the loss date and location.
