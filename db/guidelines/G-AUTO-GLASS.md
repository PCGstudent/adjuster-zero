# G-AUTO-GLASS — Auto Glass Coverage

## Scope
Glass-only losses (windshield, side, rear) on private passenger autos are
covered under **Comprehensive (AUTO-COMP)**, not Collision. A standalone glass
crack or chip with no other damage is a glass loss.

## Deductible
The GLASS-ZERO-DED endorsement waives the comprehensive deductible for glass-only
repairs (not full replacement of unrelated parts). Without the endorsement the
comprehensive deductible applies.

## Eligibility
Coverage requires an active comprehensive coverage on the loss date. Repairable
chips are preferred over replacement when the damage is outside the driver's
critical viewing area.
