# G-AUTO-THEFT — Theft & Vandalism

## Coverage
Theft of the vehicle and theft-related vandalism are comprehensive perils. A
police report is required for any theft claim before settlement.

## Documentation
Required: police report number, date of report, and a statement of the last known
location. Absence of a police report parks the claim in the information-request
loop rather than denying it.

## Recovery
If a stolen vehicle is recovered before settlement, the claim converts to a
damage/repair claim for any damage sustained during the theft.
