# G-DEDUCTIBLE — Deductibles & Limits

## Application
The applicable deductible is subtracted from the covered loss amount before
payment. Comprehensive and collision may carry different deductibles. A glass
endorsement may set the glass deductible to zero.

## Limits
Payment cannot exceed the coverage limit for the applicable coverage code. When
the loss exceeds the limit, settle at the limit and note the shortfall.

## Auto-pay ceiling
Straight-through auto-payment is permitted only up to the configured auto-pay
ceiling and only when all coverage, fraud, and confidence gates pass.
