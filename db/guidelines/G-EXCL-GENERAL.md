# G-EXCL-GENERAL — General Exclusions

## Always excluded
Intentional acts by the insured, normal wear and tear, mechanical breakdown,
manufacturing defects, and losses occurring while the vehicle is used for an
excluded commercial purpose (e.g. unlisted ride-share) are excluded.

## Burden and confidence
An exclusion should only drive a denial when it clearly applies on the facts. A
weakly-supported exclusion (low confidence) must not auto-deny; route to human
review instead.

## Concurrent causation
Where a covered and an excluded cause both contribute, apply the policy's
anti-concurrent-causation language if present; otherwise resolve in favor of
coverage for the covered cause.
