# G-INJURY-BI — Bodily Injury & Severity

## Escalation
Any claim with a bodily-injury flag, an attorney-representation flag, or a
severity rating of 4 or higher is high-severity and is escalated to a senior
adjuster; it is never straight-through processed.

## Documentation
Injury claims require medical documentation before reserve adequacy can be
assessed. Reserve conservatively pending documentation.

## Legal representation
Once an attorney is involved, all claimant communication is routed through the
adjuster and the claim leaves any automated settlement path.
