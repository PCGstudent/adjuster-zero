# G-POLICY-LAPSE — Lapse & Effective Dates

## Coverage in force
A loss is only covered if the policy was active (in force) on the date of loss.
A policy that lapsed for non-payment before the loss date provides no coverage
for that loss, even if later reinstated.

## Grace period
A statutory grace period may keep a policy in force briefly past the due date.
If the loss falls inside the grace period and payment is subsequently made, the
loss is covered. Outside the grace period, the loss is not covered.

## Reinstatement gap
Reinstatement after a lapse creates a coverage gap; losses occurring in the gap
between lapse and reinstatement are not covered.
