# G-PROP-FIRE — Property Fire & Smoke

## Coverage
Fire, smoke, and resulting soot damage to the dwelling and contents are covered
perils. Wildfire is covered unless a specific wildfire exclusion or moratorium
applies in the rated territory.

## Arson exclusion
Intentional loss caused by or at the direction of the insured (arson) is
excluded. Indicators of incendiary origin route the claim to fraud investigation.

## Contents
Contents are settled at actual cash value unless replacement-cost coverage is
endorsed. High-value items may have sub-limits.
