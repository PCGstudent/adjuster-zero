# G-PROP-WATER — Property Water Damage

## Coverage
Sudden and accidental water discharge (burst pipe, failed water heater) is
covered. Gradual leakage, seepage, and damage from long-term lack of maintenance
are excluded.

## Flood exclusion
Surface-water flooding is excluded from standard property policies and requires a
separate flood policy. Distinguish internal plumbing discharge (covered) from
external flood (excluded).

## Mitigation
The insured must take reasonable steps to mitigate further damage; unmitigated
secondary damage may be reduced at settlement.
