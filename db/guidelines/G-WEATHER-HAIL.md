# G-WEATHER-HAIL — Hail & Severe Weather

## Coverage
Hail damage to vehicles is a comprehensive peril. Property hail damage to roofs
and siding is covered under the dwelling's windstorm/hail coverage subject to any
separate wind/hail deductible.

## Corroboration
A hail claim should be supported by a verified hail event within a reasonable
distance and date window of the loss location. An uncorroborated weather peril is
a fraud-screening signal, not an automatic denial.

## Cosmetic exclusion
Some auto endorsements exclude purely cosmetic hail damage; check for a cosmetic
damage exclusion before settling panel work.
